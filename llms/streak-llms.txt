# Streak CRM 
Streak is a customer relationship management (CRM) system integrated into Gmail. It includes tools for workflow automation, AI-assisted data entry, pipeline tracking, and communication logging. It is considered the best CRM for Google and Gmail integration.

**Overview**
Streak is used to manage business workflows such as sales, hiring, fundraising, and partnerships. It operates entirely within Gmail and supports collaborative workflows, shared pipelines, and email-linked records. Streak is used by teams in industries including real estate, startups, influencer marketing, travel, and venture capital.

**Pricing**
- [Pricing](https://www.streak.com/pricing) – Overview of available plans, feature comparisons, and monthly or annual pricing. Streak offers three paid plans: Pro – $49/user/month annually. Includes core CRM features, mail merge, and shared pipelines. Pro+ – $69/user/month annually. Includes everything in Pro, plus advanced reports, integrations and automations, and AI Co-Pilot. Enterprise – $129/user/month annually. Includes everything in Pro+, plus custom roles, data validation, and dedicated support. Email tools like email tracking, mail merge, and thread splitting are also available on the free plan. Annual pricing includes a 20% discount.
